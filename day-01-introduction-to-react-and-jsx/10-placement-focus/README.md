# 🎯 Module 10: Placement Focus & Campus Interview Prep

## 💼 Overview: What Do Recruiters Look For?
For frontend and full-stack developer roles (TCS Digital, Cognizant GenC Next, Wipro Turbo, Zoho, Infosys DSE, product startups, and tech service firms), interviewers test whether candidates understand **core fundamentals** rather than just copy-pasting code.

On **Day 1**, your placement focus areas are:
1. **React Architecture & Mental Model:** Why React? How is it different from traditional DOM manipulation?
2. **SPA vs MPA:** How routing and page transitions work behind the scenes.
3. **Virtual DOM & Reconciliation:** Can you explain diffing algorithm and batching without memorized buzzwords?
4. **JSX Under the Hood:** What does JSX compile into? Why can't browsers read JSX directly?
5. **Component Concepts:** Reusability, PascalCase convention, default vs named exports, key prop necessity.
